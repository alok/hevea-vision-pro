    Definition and conventions

    Convergents are truncations of the chosen expansion. Canonical form ends in at least 2, except for an integer. The other form replaces the last term a with a−1, 1.

    Gold rings mark convergents; gray rings mark other visited mediants. Rings mark primitive lattice points. Changing the target or removing a row clears its spotlight; the camera stays still.

    Mathematics & sources

    In the ordinary lattice, each sphere is an integer pair (q,p). The fraction p/q is its direction. Neighbor vectors form a parallelogram of area one in integer coordinates; adding them gives their mediant. The finite green plane stands perpendicular to the lattice through that direction.

    Construction projects directions onto q=1. Its optional dot motion is an illustration, not a hyperbolic map. Stange's original stages move the camera around fixed lattice points.
