Explore coefficient space

Integer triples fill space around both cone halves. Upper interior: discriminant colors. Lower interior: gray. Exterior: blue. Boundary: gold.

Drag to look. W/A/S/D move; Q/E move down/up. This is ambient Euclidean coefficient space, not hyperbolic or Flatland navigation.

Exact correspondence

x = −b, y = a+c, z = c−a
Δ = b²−4ac = x²+z²−y²

The whole lattice shows integer coefficient triples in a finite ball x²+y²+z²≤R², including both signs. Turn Whole coefficient lattice off to keep only a>0, Δ<0. The full transformed grid is not arbitrary integer (x,y,z).

The optional screen y=4 cuts the upper cone in a disk. A lower-half triple is sign-flipped first; a polynomial and its negative have the same roots. Radial projection multiplies every coordinate by 4/(a+c). Multiples, such as (1,−1,2) and (2,−2,4), lie at different depths but share one image and one quadratic root.

The normalized disk coordinates u=−b/(a+c), v=(c−a)/(a+c) map to the upper half-plane by u/(1−v) + i√(1−u²−v²)/(1−v), the quadratic root shown above.

Drag to orbit · scroll to zoom · right-drag to pan. Focus the scene and use arrows to pan, +/− to zoom, Home to reset. No automatic motion.

Sources and scope

Original implementation of the coefficient-cone construction in Katherine Stange’s talk and Starscape / Lattice viewer. Our symmetric cone extent and origin-centered crop differ from the original viewer’s asymmetric framing. Its upper discriminant palette is interpolated in linear RGB here; lower-half coloring is an opt-in extension. Finite points and sphere sizes are presentation choices. No source code or media from her viewer is bundled.

The separate Farey L/R demonstration credits Caroline Series (1985). The two lattices have distinct meanings. Research background: Algebraic Number Starscapes — Harriss, Stange and Trettel.
