The coefficient cone

From three dimensions to two

Orange is an integer coefficient point. White is its image on the cyan projection disk. Other coefficient points occupy different depths inside the cone.

Exact correspondence

x = −b, y = a+c, z = c−a
Δ = b²−4ac = x²+z²−y²

We keep integer triples with a>0, Δ<0 and a+c≤8. This is a transformed coefficient lattice, not arbitrary integer points in (x,y,z).

The screen y=4 cuts the cone in a disk. Radial projection multiplies every coordinate by 4/(a+c). Multiples, such as (1,−1,2) and (2,−2,4), lie at different depths but share one image and one quadratic root.

The normalized disk coordinates u=−b/(a+c), v=(c−a)/(a+c) map to the upper half-plane by u/(1−v) + i√(1−u²−v²)/(1−v), the quadratic root shown above.
